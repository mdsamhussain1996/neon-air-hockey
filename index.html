Rules and power-ups

Keep your mallet on your own half. Hit the puck into your opponent's goal; the first player to 7 wins. The puck bounces off the walls and mallets, and a fast-moving mallet hits harder.

  • Freeze: your opponent's mallet moves at half speed for 5 seconds.
  • Multi-puck: a second puck joins the game (up to three at once).
  • Shield: a barrier covers your goal and blocks one shot (or lasts 10 seconds).

Power-ups appear in the centre zone. Whoever touched the puck last gets the power-up when the puck hits it.

Install as an app: in Chrome or Edge, use the Install app button (or the install icon in the address bar). On an iPhone or iPad, open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Online play connects the two devices directly. A free matchmaking service only introduces them. Some school or office networks block direct connections; if a game won't connect, try another Wi-Fi network or mobile data.
