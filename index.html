HOW TO PLAY

Objective & Rules

Defend your goal and smash the puck into your opponent's pocket. First player to 7 goals wins the match.

Keep your mallet on your own half of the rink. A fast-moving mallet strikes with massive impulse!

Power-ups

Hit the floating orbs in the centre zone to trigger game-changing powers:

  • ❄️ Freeze: Slows your opponent's mallet speed by 50% for 5 seconds.
  • ⚡ Multi-puck: Launches an extra puck onto the rink (up to 3 simultaneous pucks).
  • 🛡️ Shield: Barrier seals your goal to block a direct strike or lasts 10 seconds.
Controls

Touch (Mobile/Tablet): Drag anywhere on your half to guide your mallet.

Mouse & Keyboard (Laptop): Cyan steers with Mouse or Arrow Keys. Magenta uses W A S D. Press P or Esc to pause.
